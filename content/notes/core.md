## Discrepancies

1. **Species with habitat in the combined recovery footprint: 299 vs 250.** The Executive Summary gives 299 (p.vi, lines 237-238). Ch 6i gives 250 (p.81, lines 3244-3246). Ch 6i also gives 267 species for the priority footprint (line 3269), which is more than its own recovery figure. The tracker (XP-01) says 299 = 442 - 143, and 250 = 299 minus the 49 snails, which have exclosure sites but no recovery habitat. The release candidate (RC) counts 330 taxa with targets. **Site uses 299 (home.md), the Executive Summary figure.**
2. **Combined priority footprint: 216 PUs / 267 species (plan) vs 237 PUs / 305 taxa (RC).** The plan gives 216 at p.vi lines 239-240 and p.81 lines 3268-3269. The interactive map will show the RC layer, so the text and the map will disagree (XP-04, Open; PLAN.md section 5). **Site text uses 216 / 267.** DOFAW needs to decide before launch.
3. **Plant priority footprint: 181 vs 155 PUs.** App 1 gives 181 (p.A1-5 lines 5148-5150; p.A1-6 lines 5170-5171), which matches the RC. The glossary (p.122, "Priority Footprint" entry), Ch 5e and Ch 6d give 155 (XP-05). **approach.md (App 1 digest) uses 181 because it summarizes App 1.** The species and groups pages may use 155 if they follow the plan body, which would make the site inconsistent. Related: the PEPP filter is 110 PUs in App 1 (line 5174), 98 in Table 4, and 125 in the RC (XP-06). approach.md quotes App 1's 110.
4. **At-risk species totals: 442 vs 374 vs "more than 400".** 442 is used throughout. The PTM count is 374 (p.13 lines 791, 797), and the plan explains that the Taxonomic Teams later added 68 (p.14 lines 801-802). App 1 says "more than 400" (p.A1-1 line 4930). The PTM manuscript draft gives 372 species in 15 groups (XP-17). **Site uses 442, and quotes 374 only on approach.md with the plan's 68-species explanation.**
5. **Seven vs eight groups.** Ch 3b has seven teams and eight groups, the bat being the eighth (p.11 lines 704-707). App 1 has "seven focal taxonomic groups" (lines 4947, 5088, 5107). Ch 6i says the combined recovery footprint covers "all eight species groups" (line 3244), yet the bat has no footprint of its own. The site says seven teams, eight groups, and seven groups with footprints.
6. **Footprint types: three in App 1, two in the messaging guide.** App 1 lists recovery footprints, priority footprints and "implementation subsets" (p.A1-4 lines 5089-5097). Ch 6i calls the third type "Initial Implementation Areas" (line 3272). The messaging guide names only two types. approach.md leads with the two types and mentions implementation subsets only in the practitioner digest. Ch 6i also says priority footprints "are not expected to meet recovery goals" (lines 3265-3266), which reads differently from App 1's "where both preventing extinction and recovery goals can be advanced" (line 5094).

XP-26 (Resolved) found that 65.7 is the biodiversity-reserve share. **The site keeps the plan's wording ("conservation-oriented land").** DOFAW may prefer "biodiversity reserves".
